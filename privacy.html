Privacy & cookie policy

OpenLiDAR — Last updated: August 2026 · Language: English.

This policy explains how the OpenLiDAR web application ("the app", "we") handles personal data. We keep it simple by design: the app itself collects no personal data, sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics. This policy is provided for transparency and to comply with applicable data-protection law where it applies (in particular the EU General Data Protection Regulation, "GDPR", and the UK GDPR).

1. Who is responsible (data controller)

OpenLiDAR is an independent, non-commercial, open-source project. It is not a company and is not affiliated with any data provider. As the app does not collect, store or process personal data about you itself, the obligations of a "data controller" within the meaning of Art. 4(7) GDPR apply to the app's operator only in respect of the hosting arrangements described in section 5. Because we hold no personal data, most GDPR rights relating to the app are addressed purely by the local-storage controls described below.

2. Data we do not collect

  • No account. You never register, so no name, e-mail or password is ever requested.
  • No analytics or trackers. No cookies for profiling, no pixels, no measurement scripts.
  • No advertising. No ads or ad-related third-party scripts.
  • No payment data. The app is free and takes no payment.

3. Data stored on your own device

The app stores a small amount of data locally in your browser using the localStorage API (Art. 6(1)(b) — necessary for the functionality you request):

ItemPurposeHow to delete
Saved places & notesPersist your bookmarks between visitsRemove bookmarks in the app, or clear site data in your browser
Active layers & cached search resultsSpeed and restore your last viewClear site data in your browser

This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or to any server we control. You can delete it at any time via your browser's "clear site data" (or "clear storage") control.

4. Geolocation (optional)

The optional "Locate" button uses your browser's built-in Geolocation API. The resulting coordinates are handled by your browser and operating system and are used only to centre the map on your request. Web geolocation is opt-in; the browser prompts you before any location is obtained, and you may deny it. We never store or transmit your location.

5. Hosting and technical data

The app is served as a static site on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any web server, the hosting platform may process technical data connected to your request (for example your IP address, the URL requested, user-agent and the time of request) in its server logs, and may apply its own caching and security measures. Such processing is performed by the hosting provider under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review: GitHub Privacy Statement. GitHub participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; see the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

6. Third-party services

The app loads map tiles, elevation data and geocoding results from public third-party services. When your browser requests these resources, those providers may process your IP address and request details under their own policies and lawful bases. We have no control over, and no visibility into, their processing.

7. Cookies

The app does not set any cookies itself. Because no tracking or analytics cookies are set, no cookie-consent banner is required for the app's own operation. Third-party tile and API providers may however set their own cookies when your browser loads their resources; those are governed by their policies listed above. You can block or delete cookies via your browser settings at any time.

8. Your rights

Because the app holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase. If you believe otherwise, or if you wish to exercise any of your rights under the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection), you may contact us (section 11); we will respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

9. Retention

The only persistence the app performs is the local storage described in section 3, which remains until you delete it. We keep no server-side copy of any user data, because no user data is ever transmitted to us.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the current version. Material changes will be reflected here.

11. Contact

This is an independent open-source project. For any privacy question or request, please open an issue on the project's public repository (linked from the app's "About & data" panel). As we store no personal data, we ask that you do not include sensitive personal information in any message.
