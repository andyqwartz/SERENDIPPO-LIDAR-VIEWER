OpenLiDAR — Privacy & cookie policy

Last updated: August 2026.

Data collection

OpenLiDAR is a static application that runs entirely in your browser. No personal data is collected, transmitted or sold. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.

Local storage

Some preferences (saved places, active layers, cached search data) are stored locally in your browser (localStorage). They never leave your device and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.

Network calls

The app calls public third-party services to load map tiles and geocoding: IGN Géoservices, official European elevation services, OpenStreetMap, Esri, Google, CARTO, and api-adresse.data.gouv.fr. These providers operate under their own policies and may log requests to their servers.

Cookies

OpenLiDAR itself does not install any cookies. External tile and API providers may set their own cookies according to their respective policies.

Geolocation

The optional "Locate" button uses your browser's geolocation, which is only shared with your browser locally and never sent to OpenLiDAR.
