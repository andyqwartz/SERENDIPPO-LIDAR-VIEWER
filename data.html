OpenLiDAR — Sources & data

Last updated: August 2026.

OpenLiDAR is a side-by-side LiDAR / DTM relief viewer, 100% client-side, no account, no API key. It aggregates public elevation and imagery services across Europe.

Relief (LiDAR / DTM)

  • France — IGN Géoservices, WMTS/MNT hillshade, LICENCE OUVERTE 2.0. Source: IGN — updated 2026.
  • Belgium (Flanders & Wallonia) — official regional LiDAR/DTM WMS/WMTS services, open data.
  • Switzerland — swisstopo DTM/relief WMS, open data.
  • England — Environment Agency LiDAR DTM WMS, OGL v3.0.
  • Czechia, Slovakia, Luxembourg, Sweden, Norway — official national LiDAR/DTM tile services.
  • Spain (PNOA LiDAR, MDT) & Italy (PCN, TINITALY) — dev/partial coverage.

Imagery & basemaps

  • Satellite / Hybrid © Google
  • Esri World Imagery © Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics
  • CARTO basemaps © OpenStreetMap contributors © CARTO

Overlays & geocoding

  • Hydrography, cadastre, contour lines, IGN map — IGN Géoservices (LICENCE OUVERTE)
  • Search geocoding — api-adresse.data.gouv.fr & IGN Geocodage
  • All services are public endpoints referenced with an attribution by their respective providers.
